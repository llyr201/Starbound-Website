About

Pilot your starship through a neon galaxy while adapting to cosmic hazards and advanced upgrades. Join forces with other players to explore uncharted systems, customize your vessel with cutting-edge technology, and participate in epic space battles. With constantly expanding worlds and seasonal events, there's always something new to discover in Celestial Drift.

Key Features

Dynamic Combat

Experience fast-paced space combat with responsive controls and strategic depth.

Ship Customization

Unlock and customize dozens of spacecraft with unique abilities and loadouts.

Multiplayer Missions

Team up with friends to conquer challenging raids and competitive arenas.

Seasonal Events

Participate in limited-time events with exclusive rewards and new challenges.

Galactic Exploration

Discover new planets, encounter rare species, and uncover hidden secrets.

Progression System

Earn rewards, unlock cosmetics, and climb the leaderboards with your performance.
